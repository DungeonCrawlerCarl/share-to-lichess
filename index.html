Share to Lichess

Share a puzzle photo from WhatsApp or any app, scan the board on your device, open it on Lichess.

Free. No account. No upload, nothing leaves your device. No scan limit.

How to use it

  1. Install this app in Chrome on Android: menu (⋮) → Add to Home screen / Install app. It then appears in the Android share sheet as Lichess Share. Chrome only: Firefox on Android does not support share targets, so a Firefox home-screen shortcut will never appear in the share sheet. Samsung Internet and Edge on Android also work.
  2. In WhatsApp, long-press a puzzle photo → Share → Lichess Share. The photo is scanned here, on your phone.
  3. Or choose a photo below: a confident, legal read opens straight in Lichess, no stops. If the read is unsure or the position is illegal, you stay here in the board editor to fix it, then tap Open in Lichess.

Works best on 2D screenshots and diagrams (Lichess, chess.com, books, chat photos of a screen). Photos of a real wooden board taken at an angle are hit and miss; a straight overhead shot has the best chance. Accuracy on clean Lichess / chess.com renders is about 98% of positions; on real phone screenshots of other screens it is lower, so always check the board.

Side to move is read from the last-move highlight when the photo shows one (two tinted squares). Castling is assumed from king and rook on their home squares and labelled assumed, never proven. Move counters default to 0 and 1. The FEN goes to Lichess in the link URL when you tap Open in Lichess; the image itself stays on your device.

Open in Lichess Editor

“Open in Lichess” opens the Lichess analysis board. Engine off on arrival, tap the Lichess engine toggle to turn it on. That is the web board; the mobile app was not tested for this. Illegal positions open in the Lichess Editor instead. Always glance at the board before analysing: one misread piece changes the position.
